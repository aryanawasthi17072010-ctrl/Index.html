How delivery happens

Buses already run these roads every day.

  1. You book

    Pick a route and a bus. It takes about a minute.

  2. A gig worker collects

    A verified local picks the parcel up at your door.

  3. The bus carries it

    It rides an intercity bus that is already making the trip.

  4. A gig worker delivers

    Someone at the other end brings it to the doorstep.

  5. Delivered

    The status updates in tracking.
